    Halal Guidelines

    Halal: Permissible ingredients such as plant-based items, fish, and properly slaughtered Zabiha meat.

    Haram: Prohibited ingredients including pork, alcohol, carnivorous animals, and animal-derived additives from non-halal slaughter.

    Mashbooh (Doubtful): Additives or ingredients whose status depends on source origin (e.g., animal vs. synthetic enzymes).
